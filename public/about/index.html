About

About Aidfolio

Aidfolio makes free, private tools that help local and national organisations show the work they already do, and get a fairer deal from the partners who fund them.

People

Who builds it

Aidfolio is built and run by Richard Prouse in New Zealand as an independent, unpaid humanitarian project.

The code is open source under the MIT licence, and the questions and templates are free for anyone to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Contact: richard@aidfolio.org.

Privacy

Your answers stay with you

  • Nothing is uploaded. The tools run entirely in your browser. There is no server that receives your answers.
  • Saved on your device only. Answers are kept in your browser so you can continue later. On a shared computer, clear them when you finish.
  • You own the file. Save your progress as a file and open it on any device.
  • Visits are counted, people are not. Page visits are counted with a cookie-free service that never sees what you type.

Our rules

Independence

  • Local organisations never pay.
  • Paid work never changes what the free tools say or recommend, and no funder sees anyone’s answers.
  • Every paying client and every amount is listed in a public monthly ledger.
  • Aidfolio is not a registered charity, so gifts are not tax-deductible.

Common questions

Who makes Aidfolio?

Richard Prouse, in New Zealand, as an independent, unpaid humanitarian project. The code is open source on GitHub.

How is Aidfolio funded?

By small donations, grants, and paid work for international organisations that partner with local ones. Local organisations never pay, and no funder can change what the tools say. Every amount in and out is published in a monthly ledger.

Is Aidfolio a certification or an official tool?

No. It is an independent self-assessment tool. It does not certify or verify anything, and it is not affiliated with the UN, Charter for Change, Humentum or any donor.

Which languages are available?

English now. Sinhala, Tamil, French and Arabic are next, translated and tested with local organisations.
