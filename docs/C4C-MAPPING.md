# Mapping to the C4C Due Diligence Passporting Tool

Source: *Instructions for using the Charter for Change Due Diligence Passporting Tool* (Humentum and C4C, 2023), and
the C4C initiative's 2025 overview. The tool itself is a free spreadsheet from Humentum.

**Level of mapping:** capacity area. The question wording of the current spreadsheet (December 2024) was not
available to us, so question numbers below follow the published instructions and may differ in later versions.
Next step: obtain the spreadsheet and map question by question (see `OUTREACH.md`, template 4).

| C4C capacity area | Questions (instructions) | Aidfolio coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | 1 to 5 | Organisation name and country; earlier assessments (date, frameworks, or "never assessed"); registration question and certificate |
| Governance | 6 to 8 | Governance and registration: board, conflicts of interest; constitution, board list, minutes, organisation chart |
| Strategy | 9 to 10 | Strategy and accountability: strategic plan; annual or activity report |
| Accountability | 11 to 13 | Strategy and accountability: community feedback and complaints; downstream partner agreements |
| HR, Safeguarding and Security | 14 to 18 | People and security (contracts, reference checks, code of conduct, security plan); Safeguarding and PSEA (UN PSEA standards 1 to 8) |
| Financial and Asset Management | 19 to 25 | Financial management; Procurement; Assets and inventory |
| Data and IT | 26 | Data protection and IT: consent, access, retention, device and account protection, backups |
| Additional criteria | 27 onward, if agreed | Fraud, sanctions and risk (often added by agreement; some organisations cover these under Governance or Finance) |

Documents the instructions list as accompanying the tool, and where Aidfolio asks about them:
constitution (Governance), strategic plan (Strategy), complaints policy (Accountability), sample downstream
partner agreement (Accountability), HR policy, code of conduct (People), safeguarding policy (PSEA), audited
financial statements (Financial management; Aidfolio does not ask separately for the audit management
letter), finance manual (Financial management), ICT and data protection policy (Data protection and IT).

**Design rules kept from the C4C approach:**

- No self-scoring. The 1 to 4 ratings are given by the passporting organisation.
- The organisation owns its pack and decides whether to share it.
- Assessments are a development step with an action plan, not a pass or fail.

Aidfolio is independent and not affiliated with Charter for Change or Humentum.
